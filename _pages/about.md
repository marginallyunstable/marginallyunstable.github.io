Hi, I am a PhD Student withing the Dynamics Design Lab at Ghent University advised by Tom Lefebvre and Guillaume Crevecoeur. I’m endlessly intrigued by the unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics in explaining and shaping the world around us. My current interests lie at the intersection of learning and control, where I aim to develop principled methods with real-world, human-centered applications. I am also keenly interested in the democratization of knowledge and reducing the barriers to access and understanding. When I'm not geeking out, I can be found cooking, cycling, woodworking or spiraling down the the rabbit hole of random YouTube videos.
