Hoi! I'm a PhD Student within the [Dynamics Design Group](https://dynamics.ugent.be/) at Ghent University advised by [Tom Lefebvre](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=uGTVGgQAAAAJ&hl=nl) and [Guillaume Crevecoeur](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dM1WEaQAAAAJ&hl=en). I’m endlessly intrigued by the unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics in explaining and shaping the world around us. My research broadly focuses on decision-making under uncertainty for robots, integrating ideas from machine learning, modeling, control, and numerical optimization. More recently, I’ve been especially interested in the problem of contact-implicit planning by bridging techniques from *probabilistic* optimal control and reinforcement learning. I aim to develop principled methods that translate to real-world, human-centered applications.

Beyond research, I care deeply about democratizing knowledge and reducing barriers to access and understanding. When I’m not geeking out over robotics, you’ll likely find me cooking, cycling, woodworking, or spiraling down the rabbit hole of random YouTube videos.
